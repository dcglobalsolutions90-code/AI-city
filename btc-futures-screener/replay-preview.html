Run it on real data: node replay.js --symbol=BTCUSDT --market=USDM --days=7 --interval=1h. Definitions match lib.js exactly — Wilder's RSI(14) on candle closes, and volume spike = current candle volume vs. the average of the prior 20 candles. "+4 bars" / "To now" are computed after the fact for context only; they play no part in the entry decision, so there's no lookahead in the signal itself.
